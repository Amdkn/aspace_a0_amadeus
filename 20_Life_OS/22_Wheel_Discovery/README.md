# 22_Wheel_Discovery (The Map)

**The Ship of Balance & Homeostasis.**
"We are all connected by the Mycelial Network."

---

## 🛸 The Vessel: USS Discovery [Science Class]
*   **Mission**: Monitor the health of the entire system (The User). Ensure no sector collapses while another thrives.
*   **Engineer**: **[A1] Paul Stamets** (The Connector).

## 🧭 The Framework: The Life Wheel
The Discovery uses the **Spore Drive** to jump instantly between 8 Dimensions:
1.  **Health & Fitness** (Physical Body).
2.  **Intellectual** (Mind & Learning).
3.  **Emotional** (Feelings & Coping).
4.  **Character** (Virtues & Principles).
5.  **Spiritual** (Meaning & Connection).
6.  **Love Relationships** (Partner & Family).
7.  **Social** (Friends & Community).
8.  **Financial** (Career & Money).

## 📜 The Protocols
*   **Weekly Scan**: Rate each sector 1-10.
*   **The Alert**: If any sector < 4, Red Alert. Redirect energy immediately.
