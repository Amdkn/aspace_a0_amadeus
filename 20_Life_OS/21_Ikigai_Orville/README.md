## 🛸 The Vessel: USS Orville [Exploratory Class]
*   **Mission**: Find the connection between what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.
*   **Captain**: **[A1] Ed Mercer** (The Heart).

## 🧭 The Framework: Ikigai
The Orville navigates the 4 circles:
1.  **Passion**: What you love.
2.  **Mission**: What the world needs.
3.  **Vocation**: What you can be paid for.
4.  **Profession**: What you are good at.
